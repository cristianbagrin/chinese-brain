A card opens with the word's Taiwan pinyin, meaning and example sentences.

Stamp

Press 1 新 Fresh, 2 學 Learning or 3 熟 Known. Your words get their color everywhere.

Watch

Open a Chinese video on YouTube: Chinese and English lines appear, and every word is hoverable.

Select anything

Select a name, phrase or sentence and tap Alt (⌥ Option on a Mac) for its pinyin and translation.

Your word list

Toolbar button → Word list: review, restamp, import, export. Every shortcut can be changed in Settings.

Sync (optional)

Create a free account in Settings and your words follow you to every browser.
