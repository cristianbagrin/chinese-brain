What's new

Chinese Brain updates itself. Here's what that got you.

  1. Nov 18

    注音, the way Taiwan reads

    Every reading is now in 注音, right beside each character, like a Taiwanese schoolbook. The card, subtitles, transcript and Dashboard all switch together. Prefer pinyin? One click in the toolbar menu.

    中文
  2. Nov 18

    One Dashboard

    Your words live in one place: the Dashboard. It opens on your latest 30; Show more brings the next 100.

  3. Nov 18

    The card, your way on each website

    Hover on most sites, Alt on the busy ones, or the other way round. Pick it for this website from the toolbar menu.

  4. Nov 17

    Chinese Brain is on Chrome

    Install it from the Chrome Web Store, in Chrome, Edge, Brave, Arc or any browser built on Chrome. Same card, same stamps, same list. Sign in on both and they share one list.

    Add to Chrome Add to Firefox
  5. Nov 16

    Your words, on every screen

    Word list now opens on the website. With the extension, it reads straight from your browser: no account, nothing uploaded. Signed in, the same list is on your phone.

    新12學34熟210
  6. Nov 16

    New words, one click away

    The toolbar menu downloads everything you stamped since last time, ready for Anki or a spreadsheet.

  7. Nov 16

    Off means off

    Switch colors off on a website and it stays off, even with colors on everywhere else.

  8. Nov 15

    Practice with any AI

    Copy prompt turns the words you're learning into a short story and a quiz. Paste it into Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini.

    Write a short story with 夜市, 捷運, 方便… then quiz me.

  9. Nov 15

    See how much of a page you can read

    Your words keep their color on every website. One glance and you know.
